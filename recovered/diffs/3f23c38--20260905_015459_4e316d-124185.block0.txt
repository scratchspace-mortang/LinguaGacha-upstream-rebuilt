    fix(backend): bound data: URL diagnostic output to 256-char max
    
    Previously url_diagnostic() returned the full data: URL meta-prefix
    (up to the first comma) without any length cap. A malicious or
    pathological data: URL with a very long meta section (e.g.
    data:text/html;charset=utf-8;base64,<very long>) could bloat
    stderr logs and leak content into diagnostic output.
    
    Now: no-comma data URLs return INVALID_DATA_URL_HEAD ("<invalid>")
    instead of the full href; valid meta-prefix is capped at 256 chars.
    
    P2 finding from codex review of 0fa13e4.
